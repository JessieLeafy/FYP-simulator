---
name: tighten-section
description: Improve clarity and conciseness of an academic section without changing technical meaning.
---

When invoked:

1. Read the target section carefully.
2. Remove redundancy and overly long sentences.
3. Preserve all technical claims, citations, and numbers.
4. Keep the tone appropriate for an academic conference paper.
5. Do not invent facts or references.

Output:
- revised text
- short summary of the main improvements
- any items that need manual factual verification
